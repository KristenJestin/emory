# reads it there, and appends the words the mind adds once the user said yes).

# `#` starts a comment. With at least one word, the beam search replaces the greedy one (about a
# fifth to a half more compute per sentence, see specs/hearing.md); an empty file keeps the
# greedy search. Case matters to the model: write each name as it should come out.
#
# Keep to names that are not words of the language: a French word here (« trafic ») would be
# pulled out of sentences that do not mean it. A form the model hears too far from the name (« Et
# Mori » for Emory) is not rescued by this list: that is the corrections table's job.
